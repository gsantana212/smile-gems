HOW IT WORKS

From box to bling in 90 seconds.

No dentist appointment. No $200 salon session. Just clean, isolate, etch, place, cure.

1

Clean

Brush, floss, isolate the tooth with a cotton roll. Dry completely. The bonding won't hold if the tooth is wet.

2

Etch

Apply a tiny dot of 37% phosphoric acid etchant to the tooth. Wait 20 seconds. Rinse. Dry. The surface is now micro-rough.

3

Place

Drop a pinhead of bonding adhesive. Press the gem into place with a microbrush. Hold steady for 5 seconds.

4

Cure

Shine the LED cure light for 20 seconds. The composite hardens. The gem is locked. Don't eat for 30 minutes.

FAQ

Questions. Answered.

If yours isn't here, text us: hi@skillhub.shop

Is this safe for my teeth?

Yes. We use the same 37% phosphoric acid etchant and light-cure composite that dentists use for sealants. The gem sits on top of the enamel — nothing drills or removes tooth structure. When you're ready, the gem pops off and the enamel underneath is unchanged.

How long does a gem last?

2–8 weeks typical, depending on diet and bite. Coffee, red wine, and biting into hard food (apples, crusty bread) shorten it. Avoid those for the first 24 hours and you'll get the upper end.

Can I do this on composite bonding or veneers?

Yes — the same process works on bonding, veneers, and crowns. Etch the surface, apply composite, place gem, cure. Many of our buyers are repeat customers who want gems on existing dental work.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. US, Canada, UK, EU, AU. The kit contains small quantities of dental chemicals (etchant, composite) which we ship as a "dental kit" so customs won't flag it. Delivery in 5–10 business days.

What if I mess up the application?

Send us a photo. We'll either send a replacement crystal or refund you. Most "messed up" applications are actually fine — they just need the light-cure step done properly. The training video walks through every step.

Can I resell these as an artist?

Yes. The Vault kit is built for this. Many of our customers charge $80–$150 per client and pay off the kit in 1–2 sessions. We don't take a cut, we don't brand your work. It's yours.
